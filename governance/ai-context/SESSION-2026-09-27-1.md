`textContent` dump showed "Add Student"/"View/Manage" already present, which
was **wrongly read** as "no expand click needed" — the expand click was
removed. That also failed live, and the user confirmed by hand that the row
genuinely does need expanding. The `textContent` dump was worthless as
evidence because it includes CSS-hidden text regardless of visibility. Both
adapter and fixture were reverted. The restored click *still* failed a third
time — it hit the row's bounding-box center, which on a wide multi-column
table is a different column than the class name. Re-reading the source
recording frame by frame showed the cursor on the class-name text; fixed to
click that cell.

Recorded in full because the back-and-forth is itself the evidence that
selectors were never guessed into place.
